# Playing Purple Enel Efficiently — A Pilot's Guide

**Leader:** Enel (OP15-058) · Mono-**Purple** · Sky Island · 5 Life · 5000 power
**Deckbuilder source:** https://deckbuilder.egmanevents.com/optcg/deck

---

## 1. What this deck actually is

Enel is **not** a ramp-into-big-things deck. It's a **cheap, draw-heavy tempo deck** built on one rule printed on the Leader:

> *Under the rules of this game, your DON!! deck consists of **6 cards**.*

Because you can never have more than 6 DON!! on the field, you don't try to out-resource the opponent. You **out-draw and out-tempo** them. Almost every card you play either draws a card, recycles a DON!!, or both — so you see a huge chunk of your deck, never run out of gas, and apply constant pressure while a sticky **Enel (8000)** finishes the game.

**Your win condition:** chip the opponent's Life down with a wide board of cheap, DON-boosted attackers, protect a hard-to-remove Enel, and close before they stabilize.

---

## 2. The three engines you're looping

Everything in the deck feeds one of three engines. Efficient play = keeping all three turning.

### Engine A — Card draw
- **Pudding, Ohm, Satori, Shura** are all **1-cost 2000 bodies that draw a card** when played (On Play: DON!! −1, draw 1).
- **El Thor, Lightning Beast Kiten, Lightning Dragon, Varie!!** are **1-cost events that draw a card** while doing something else.
- Net effect: you can play 3–4 cards a turn and still keep a full hand.

### Engine B — The DON!! loop
- "**DON!! −1**" means you **return a DON!! from your field to your DON!! deck** as part of the cost.
- That refills your 6-card DON!! deck, so next turn's DON!! Phase can place them again.
- This is why the small DON!! deck isn't a problem: you're constantly cycling the same DON!! through draw spells.

### Engine C — DON!! attaching (your damage)
- Your Leader ability (turn 2+) lets you set DON!! and **give them to Characters: +1000 power each, for the turn**.
- Giving 3 DON!! to a 7000 Enel = a **10000** attacker. This is where your damage comes from, so **save attaching for the turn you actually swing.**

## 3. The efficient turn sequence

Run this checklist every turn:

1. **Draw step** — note what you drew; it shapes the turn.
2. **DON!! step** — you place 2 (1 on the play's first turn). Know your DON!! budget before you commit.
3. **Lead with a draw card** — play Shura/Pudding/Ohm or a 1-cost event *first*. Drawing before you decide the rest of the turn means you make the turn with full information.
4. **Develop / interact** — drop bodies, and use removal **on blockers before you attack**, never after.
5. **Attach, then attack** — give DON!! to your attackers only once you're ready to swing, and attack in the right order (see §6).
6. **End** with your hand reloaded and your board threatening.

> **Rule of thumb:** if you ended a turn with a hand that didn't grow and a board that didn't threaten, you sequenced inefficiently.

## 5. Making Enel stick (the most important interaction)

Your 8000 Enel **can't be removed by opponent effects while you have 4 or fewer DON!!**.

- After you play Enel, **don't over-ramp.** Keep your DON!! count at 4 or below and their removal events simply can't touch it.
- Because your DON!! deck is only 6, staying at ≤4 is natural — but be deliberate about it on the turn you land Enel.
- An un-removable 8000 that you can also pump with attached DON!! is your game-ender. Defend it with your own tempo and let it swing every turn.

---

## 6. Combat efficiency (sequencing your attacks)

The order you attack in matters more than people think:

1. **Remove blockers first** — Gamma Knife / El Thor / Ohm's attack trigger. Swinging into an unknown blocker wastes an attack.
2. **Attack with your *weakest qualifying* attacker first** when you just need to force a block — make them use a blocker on a small hit, then send the big ones at the Leader.
3. **Attack rested characters** you want to kill *before* they can be used to block.
4. **Pump last** — only attach DON!! once you've decided which attacker needs to cross 5000 (or out-muscle a blocker). Don't pre-commit DON!! you might want elsewhere.

**Remember the life rule:** a hit on the Leader sends their top Life card to **their hand** (it doesn't kill). You only win by hitting a Leader that has **0 Life left**. So beating an opponent on 2 Life requires **3** connecting attacks in total — plan your lethal turn around `Life + 1` hits getting through their blockers.

## 7. Mulligan

Keep almost any hand with a **1-cost play** — ideally **Shura**, because you draw into everything else fast.

- **Snap keep:** Shura + any other 1-drop, or a 1-drop + a cheap event.
- **Keep:** any hand with two early plays.
- **Mulligan:** all 4–6 drops, no early plays. With a 6-card DON!! deck you can't reliably power those out early, and you'll fall behind on tempo.

You do **not** need removal in your opener — you'll dig to it.

## 8. Matchup plans

**vs Aggro / other fast decks**
- Trade early and often: Ohm, El Thor, Gamma Knife to break their board.
- Land Enel and keep ≤4 DON!! so it can't be removed — it becomes a wall that also attacks.
- Win the long game on raw card advantage; you refill, they don't.

**vs Control / Big-stuff decks**
- **You are the beatdown.** Curve out bodies, draw relentlessly, and pressure their Life before they set up.
- Don't dump your whole board into an obvious board-wipe turn — your draw engine means you can rebuild, so hold one threat back.
- Use Lightning Dragon's lock to deny their key blocker the turn you push for lethal.

---

## 9. Five habits of an efficient Enel pilot

1. **Draw before you decide.** Always play a draw card early in the turn.
2. **Recycle DON!! on purpose.** Lead with "DON!! −1" cards so your deck stays fed.
3. **Attach late.** DON!! pumps are for the swing, not the setup.
4. **Clear, then hit.** Removal goes on blockers *before* attacks, every time.
5. **Protect the god.** Land Enel, sit at ≤4 DON!!, and ride it to the win.
